The board shows a task's `flag`. A flagged card gets a warning border and its reason, and columns other than the done column count flagged cards in their header. A `Flagged` toggle in the header narrows the board to flagged tasks. The detail view sets, rewords, and clears a flag. Read-only hosts show the flag with no controls. A flag on a done task stays visible in neutral colours.
